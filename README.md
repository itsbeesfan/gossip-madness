## gossip madness!
### gossip spreads about you at the sleepover. how do you choose to react: do you stand your ground confidently, or do you run away from conflict?
### a game made for sleepover's "snoozefest"!

### what is this?
this is a story game in which you act as noelle, a highschooler at a sleepover, who gets involved in a rumor and some serious acussations
about... _smelly feet!_ 

you'll have to take decisions that might lead you to be confident and end the rumors, or that might lead you to losing your popularity and remembering
that night... _forever..._

### ok but what are the features?

- **confidence and reputation stats:** depending on your choices, these will go up or down!
- **mood changing:** your avatar (bottom right) will change expressions depending on the confidence it has based on the decisions you make!
- **appearing text:** you know how in story games text slowly appears and it lets you read it? even in pokemon? yes, this game has that!
- **three different spaces:** the story takes place in three different scenes: a sleepover bedroom, a bathroom, and a backyard!
- **custom art:** yes, all art IS made by me. id say its good work.
- **sfx and music:** yes, the cherry on top. not much to explain about this to be honest.
